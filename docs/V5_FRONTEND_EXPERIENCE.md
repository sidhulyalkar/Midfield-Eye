# v0.5 Frontend Experience

## Product thesis

A viewer should leave the application understanding that midfield excellence is not one action. It is the construction, perception, preservation, and direction of many possible actions.

## Signature interaction

The user scrubs through a possession. Three synchronized canvases update:

1. Tactical Pitch: pressure, players, movement, options, and uncertainty.
2. Perception Lens: foveal, actionable, and peripheral fields plus scan events.
3. Relational Graph: teammate support, opponent attraction, and option enablement.

A body-mechanics strip beneath the pitch shows posture, movement heading, weight-transfer proxy, and action readiness.

## Atlas experience

The 100-player atlas should feel exploratory rather than encyclopedic:

- filter by tactical question;
- compare players by role and context;
- open a player's research hypothesis;
- inspect the evidence needed to validate it;
- launch available scenarios;
- pin players to a relational comparison.

## Visual standard

- SVG pitch and player cards;
- DPR-aware Canvas for dense animation;
- crisp 4K export mode;
- no stock-dashboard pie charts;
- no unlicensed player photography;
- clear hierarchy around the pitch;
- muted analytical panels with bright option and gaze signals;
- keyboard and reduced-motion support;
- meaningful loading skeletons and empty states.
