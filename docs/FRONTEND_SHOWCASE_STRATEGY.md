# Frontend Showcase Strategy

## The opening story

The app should begin with a single possession and one sentence:

> The pass is the ending. The opportunity began several seconds earlier.

The viewer first sees a normal pitch state. As the timeline moves, passing corridors brighten, pressure shadows drift, a teammate's off-ball run changes defender reachability, and the top option switches. The interface then rewinds and asks what would have happened if the supporting player moved two metres earlier.

## Four signature perspectives

### 1. The player's eye

Display the carrier's estimated field of view, orientation, visible options, and potentially valuable options outside the visible cone. This is the perceptual layer.

### 2. The coach's eye

Translate the model into instructions:

- Open your hips earlier
- Hold the defender for one more touch
- Arrive in the pocket after the pass starts
- Move five metres to improve the next player's options

### 3. The analyst's eye

Expose feature values, uncertainty, provider quality, option ranks, temporal stability, and counterfactual deltas.

### 4. The perception engineer's eye

Show how camera crop, calibration drift, missing players, and identity switches alter tactical conclusions.

## Showcase sequence

1. Synthetic scenario to teach the interface
2. Open tracking example to establish empirical grounding
3. Broadcast reconstruction example to show real-world difficulty
4. Named-player study with rights-cleared evidence
5. Counterfactual coaching intervention
6. Reliability and limitations

## Portfolio outputs

Generate:

- Interactive website
- 4K tactical cards
- 30–60 second screen recording
- One-page technical architecture
- One-page player study
- Research protocol and limitations
- Reproducible analysis bundle

## What makes the project distinctive

The differentiation is not player tracking alone. It is the coupling of:

- Partial perception
- Dynamic action menus
- Uncertainty
- Temporal option creation
- Counterfactual positioning
- Coaching-language explanation

That combination gives the project a clear identity across sports analytics, computer vision, embodied intelligence, and human decision modeling.
