# 100 Elite Midfielder Study Atlas

## Purpose

The atlas provides a broad, balanced set of tactical research subjects for the frontend and experiment system. It contains 50 candidates from the men's game and 50 from the women's game.

It is not an ordinal ranking. There is no scientifically defensible single scale that compares a holding midfielder, an attacking midfielder, a wide creator, and a box-to-box player across competitions, tactical roles, providers, and both games.

The atlas therefore stores:

- a role description;
- a primary tactical archetype;
- a unique qualitative signature;
- skill and talent lenses;
- gaze questions;
- body-mechanics questions;
- adaptation and orchestration questions;
- a ten-axis showcase-emphasis shape;
- evidence and profile status;
- scenario availability.

## Showcase-emphasis policy

The ten values in `showcase_emphasis` are **interface and study-design priors**. They indicate what a player page should foreground. They are not player ratings.

The axes are:

- scanning;
- gaze timing;
- body mechanics;
- adaptation;
- tempo direction;
- orchestration;
- manipulation;
- off-ball contribution;
- physical execution;
- final action.

A real comparison must replace these priors with context-normalized measurements and confidence intervals.

## Frontend behavior

The `/atlas` page must:

- display all 100 profiles without ordinal numbers;
- support cohort, role, archetype, and lens filters;
- use the supplied SVG cards or data-driven replacements;
- allow two to four players to be pinned to a comparison tray;
- display `Editorial research hypothesis` on every unmeasured profile;
- surface scenario availability and evidence status;
- never imply that an emphasis polygon is a radar rating.

## Recommended cohort views

- controlling pivots;
- pressure-resistant connectors;
- half-space creators;
- dynamic eights;
- transition controllers;
- off-ball space manipulators;
- high-frequency support players;
- final-action creators;
- role-adaptive midfielders;
- emerging players.

## Evidence upgrade path

A player moves from `hypothesis_only` to `mixed` or `measured` only when the repository contains:

1. rights-cleared footage or licensed tracking;
2. match and competition context;
3. a reproducible perception and reconstruction manifest;
4. annotation or event linkage;
5. uncertainty estimates;
6. comparison-context filters;
7. a claim record identifying what was and was not measured.
